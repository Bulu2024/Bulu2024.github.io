The Problem

Blind Spots Cost Experience

Operators see counters, not experience. Element managers report that a cell is "up" while subscribers on it are buffering, timing out, and dropping calls. Traffic keeps growing, encryption hides the application mix, and congestion moves faster than manual analysis can follow.

LonalytIQ closes the gap between "the network is running" and "the network feels fast," measuring quality as the subscriber actually experiences it.

The Visibility Gap

Node-level KPIs tell you a link is 80% utilized. They do not tell you that video start delay tripled on that path last Tuesday at 8pm, or which backhaul segment caused it. LonalytIQ correlates the two automatically.

Our Solution

Complete Network Analytics Suite

Quality of Experience Scoring

Continuous per-application QoE measurement across the network: video MOS and start-up delay, rebuffering ratio, voice MOS for VoLTE/VoWiFi, web page load times, and TCP/DNS handshake latency. See exactly which services are degrading and where, before the complaints arrive.

End-to-End Network Visibility

Full-stack flow visibility across access, backhaul, core, and peering. Map which paths carry which traffic, correlate flows across every layer, and follow a session from the radio all the way to the upstream CDN in a single view.

Congestion & Bottleneck Detection

Automatically surface congested cells, saturated backhaul links, and overloaded gateways. Correlate utilization spikes with QoE drops to determine whether the bottleneck is radio, transport, core, or upstream peering, and rank issues by subscriber impact.

Congestion Path Monitoring

Track how congestion propagates across the topology over time. Visualize time-of-day patterns, recurring hotspots, and the exact links contributing to degradation, so planning teams act on trends rather than isolated alarms.

Optimization Recommendations

Turn visibility into action: capacity-planning guidance, traffic-steering and load-balancing opportunities, CDN and cache-placement insights, peering optimization, and offload candidates for Wi-Fi and small cells, each quantified by expected experience gain.

Application & Traffic Mix Analytics

Understand the composition of traffic driving your capacity needs. Track the share and growth of video, social, gaming, and cloud services, and forecast where demand is heading so investment lands where it matters.

Experience Segmentation

Compare how different device types, service tiers, and usage profiles experience the network. Verify premium SLA adherence, spot underserved segments, and understand heavy-user impact, all in aggregate without touching individual content.

Geospatial Performance Mapping

See QoE and congestion on the map. Identify geographic zones with poor video quality, high latency, or recurring congestion, and overlay them with cell and backhaul topology to prioritize where investment delivers the most improvement.

Real-Time Dashboards & KPIs

A single pane of glass for network operations and planning. Live dashboards surface the KPIs that matter, MOS, PRB and link utilization, latency, jitter, packet loss, throughput per subscriber, and a composite congestion index, with drill-down from nationwide to node level.

Proactive Alerting & Anomaly Detection

Set thresholds on experience and utilization metrics and get notified the moment a cell, link, or service crosses them. Baseline-driven anomaly detection flags unusual degradation automatically, shortening time-to-detection from hours to seconds.

AI & Machine Learning Core

An analytics engine that does more than report. Machine learning forecasts capacity exhaustion, detects abnormal experience patterns, correlates QoE drops to root-cause locations, and features a natural-language query layer that turns billions of flow records into clear visual answers.

Integration & Data Sources

Fit into the existing OSS/BSS stack. Ingest from probes, DPI, and network element managers, and expose results through open APIs and direct database connections so QoE and congestion insight flows into planning, assurance, and reporting tools.
